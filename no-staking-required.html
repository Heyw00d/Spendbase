Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some crypto cards require staking? ▼

Companies like Crypto.com use staking to create demand for their native tokens. By requiring you to buy and lock CRO/PLU/etc., they prop up token prices. It's a business model that benefits them more than you.

What happens if I want to unstake early? ▼

Most cards don't allow early unstaking. Your tokens are locked for the full period (usually 6 months). Even if the token crashes 90%, you can't sell until the lock expires. You'll also lose card benefits immediately upon unstaking.

Are no-stake card rewards really as good? ▼

Yes! Cards like Fold (up to 4.5% in BTC), Coinbase (up to 4%), and others offer competitive rewards without any staking. Plus, you're not gambling thousands on volatile tokens — so your effective return is often better.

What about the "free" Netflix/Spotify perks from staking cards? ▼

Those perks cost $10-15/month. To get them, you stake $4,000+ in volatile tokens. If that stake loses 50%, you've lost $2,000 to "save" $150/year on streaming. The math rarely works out.
