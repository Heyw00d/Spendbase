Frequently Asked Questions

Is spending USDC really tax-free? ▼

Not exactly "tax-free," but typically there's no capital gain. Since USDC is pegged 1:1 to USD, you buy at $1 and spend at $1. No gain = nothing to report. However, always consult a tax professional as rules vary by jurisdiction and situation.

What if USDC depegs? ▼

USDC is issued by Circle and backed by cash and short-term treasuries. While temporary depegs are possible (like March 2023), USDC has always returned to $1. For daily spending amounts, the risk is minimal. Don't store your life savings in any single asset.

USDC or USDT — which is better for cards? ▼

USDC has better regulatory standing and transparency (US-regulated, audited reserves). More cards support USDC directly. USDT has more liquidity but less regulatory clarity. For spending purposes, USDC is generally preferred.

Can I get rewards when spending USDC? ▼

Yes! Cards like Coinbase offer up to 4% cashback when spending USDC. Some DeFi cards let you earn yield on your balance until you spend. The rewards are comparable to (or better than) traditional credit cards.
