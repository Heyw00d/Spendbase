Frequently Asked Questions

Why aren't crypto cards available in my country? ▼

Most crypto cards are issued by US or EU banks, limiting where they can operate. Regulatory requirements also vary significantly — some countries have outright banned crypto products. However, new providers are emerging specifically for underserved markets.

Can I use a VPN to get a card in an unsupported country? ▼

Not recommended. VPN workarounds may let you sign up, but KYC verification will eventually require documents from your actual country. You risk getting your account frozen with funds locked inside. Look for cards that legitimately support your region.

Which crypto card has the widest global coverage? ▼

Cards like Wirex, RedotPay, and Bybit Card tend to have broader coverage than traditional exchange cards. They work in 150+ countries. Use Spendbase to filter specifically by your country.

What if I travel frequently between countries? ▼

Look for cards with no foreign transaction fees and multi-currency support. Most crypto cards work anywhere Visa/Mastercard is accepted for spending — the regional restrictions typically apply to issuance and KYC, not actual usage.
