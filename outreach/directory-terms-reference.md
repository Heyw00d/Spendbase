## Common Patterns Across All Directories

### What You're Typically Agreeing To:
1. **Content License:** You keep ownership, but grant them rights to display/modify
2. **Accuracy:** You warrant your info is accurate and you own it
3. **Removal:** They can remove listings at discretion
4. **No Guarantees:** "AS IS" service, no promises on traffic/results
5. **Terms Changes:** They can update terms without notice

### What They Cannot Do:
1. Claim ownership of your product/brand
2. Use your content for purposes beyond the directory
3. (Usually) share your private contact info publicly

### Red Flags to Watch For:
- ❌ Exclusive licensing (you can't list elsewhere)
- ❌ Payment commitments beyond stated fees
- ❌ Automatic renewals for paid tiers
- ❌ Non-compete clauses
- ❌ Mandatory promotional requirements

**None of the directories reviewed have these red flags.**

## Recommendation

All reviewed directories have **standard, low-risk terms**. Main considerations:

1. **SaaSHub** uses CC BY-SA 4.0 for alternatives lists - your product comparisons could be remixed by others
2. **All directories** reserve right to remove listings - don't rely on any single one
3. **Paid tiers** (BetaList $129, Launching Next $99) - check refund policies before paying

**Safe to proceed with submissions.** No contracts requiring legal review.
