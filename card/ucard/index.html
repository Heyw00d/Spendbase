Overview

UCard is Uchain's crypto debit card that instantly converts UCN tokens to local fiat currency when you swipe or tap. Part of the broader Uchain ecosystem including UWallet and UDefender (NFC hardware wallet). "Debit Card 3.0" launching February 28, 2026 promises enhanced features.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UCard?
UCard is Uchain's crypto debit card that instantly converts UCN tokens to local fiat currency when you swipe or tap. Available in 100+ countries with €10,000 daily limits.
What are UCard's spending limits?
UCard offers up to €10,000 daily and €100,000 monthly spending limits.
What is UDefender?
UDefender is Uchain's NFC hardware wallet for secure cold storage. Part of your mnemonic phrase lives on a physical chip card, and you confirm transactions by tapping the card on your smartphone.
Where can I use UCard?
UCard works in 100+ countries wherever major card networks are accepted.
