Overview

Switch Reward Card positions itself as a next-generation payment solution bridging everyday spending with decentralized finance. Users manage funds in both fiat and crypto (Bitcoin, Ethereum), earning "Switch Rewards" through card usage, node operation, and referrals. The platform emphasizes ease of use compared to typical crypto exchanges.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Switch Reward Card?
Switch Reward Card is a blockchain-based financial ecosystem that combines a crypto debit card with a unique rewards system. Users can hold fiat and crypto, earn rewards through various activities, and spend globally.
Is Switch Reward Card available now?
The card is currently in US alpha testing with broader availability planned. Check their website for waitlist options.
Are my funds insured?
No. Digital assets are held by third-party custodians (Ibanera LLC, Vault IST DMCC) and are not FDIC insured. As with all crypto, value can fluctuate.
What are Switch Rewards worth?
Switch Rewards are designed for use within the Switch ecosystem and marketplace. They are not sold as investment products and may not hold value outside the platform.
